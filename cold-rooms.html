Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a cold room and a freezer room?

Cold rooms are designed for chilling and typically operate between 1°C and 5°C, making them ideal for fresh produce, dairy, beverages, and flowers. Freezer rooms operate at much lower temperatures—usually -18°C to -30°C—to keep meat, seafood, and frozen goods solid for long-term storage.

Can you build a unit to fit an awkward or tight space?

Yes. Because we use modular insulated panels, we custom-manufacture every room to your exact floor plan and ceiling height. We can build around pillars, sloping floors, and tight back-of-house corners.

What power supply do I need for a walk-in unit?

Smaller cold rooms can often run on standard 220V single-phase power, making them suitable for small shops or residential spaces. Larger commercial cold rooms and deep-freeze units usually require 380V three-phase power to run the compressors efficiently. We will assess your site's electrical capacity before quoting.

How long does installation take?

Once your custom panels and refrigeration units are manufactured, on-site installation typically takes 1 to 3 days for standard commercial sizes. Larger industrial warehouses or multi-room setups may take up to a week.

Do you supply DIY kits, or do you handle the full installation?

We offer both. If you have your own qualified refrigeration technician, we can supply a complete flat-pack DIY kit with panels, doors, and matching cooling units. Otherwise, our team provides a full turnkey service from site measurement to final commissioning.

What happens during a power outage or load shedding?

High-density polyurethane panels act like a heavy-duty cooler box, keeping internal temperatures stable for several hours as long as the doors remain closed. We can also integrate backup generator changeovers, solar-compatible compressors, or temperature-alarm systems to protect your stock.

Do you offer maintenance and warranties?

Yes. All new installations come with a comprehensive workmanship and manufacturer warranty. We also offer scheduled preventative maintenance plans to keep your coils clean, gas levels optimal, and electricity bills low.
