About Us

Wenointed is a South African service provider specialising in professional refrigeration and air conditioning solutions for residential and commercial clients. We are committed to delivering reliable, efficient and affordable services to homeowners and businesses.

What We Do

Our services include fridge and freezer repairs, cold room installations and maintenance, commercial refrigeration servicing, ice machine repairs, refrigerated truck repairs and emergency breakdown assistance.


We offer air conditioning installation, servicing, repairs and gas refills to help keep residential and commercial spaces comfortable throughout the year.

Our Commitment

At Wenointed, we value professionalism, quality workmanship and customer satisfaction. Our goal is to provide dependable services that meet our clients’ needs while building lasting relationships through trust and consistent service delivery.

Areas We Serve

Based in Olievenhoutbosch, Centurion, we provide services throughout Pretoria, Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Wenointed — Your Trusted Partner in Cleaning, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning Services.
