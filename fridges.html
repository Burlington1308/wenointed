Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to move my fridge or bring it to a workshop?

No. All diagnostics, servicing, and repairs are carried out directly in your kitchen, saving you the hassle and cost of transporting a heavy appliance.

Is it better to repair my fridge or buy a new one?

If your fridge is under 10 years old and the repair costs less than half the price of a new unit, repairing it is usually the most cost-effective choice. Our technicians will always give you honest advice if a replacement makes better financial sense.

How much does a fridge repair cost?

We charge a standard call-out and diagnostic fee. Once we find the exact fault, we give you a full, upfront quote for parts and labour before doing any repair work. In most cases, the call-out fee is waived if you go ahead with the repair.
