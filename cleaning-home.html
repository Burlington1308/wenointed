Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No. Our teams arrive fully equipped with commercial-grade vacuums, microfiber cloths, mops, and eco-friendly cleaning solutions. If you prefer us to use a specific product you already own, just let us know!

Can you clean our office or commercial space outside of business hours?

Absolutely. We offer flexible early morning, evening, and weekend scheduling so our cleaning team never interrupts your staff or customers.

Do I need to be home during a residential cleaning?

Not at all. Most of our clients provide a spare key, door code, or lockbox instructions. Your access details are kept strictly confidential and secure.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on the square footage of your space, the type of cleaning required (standard, deep, or commercial), and how often you book. Recurring clients enjoy discounted rates. Request a free quote for an exact, transparent estimate with no hidden fees.

What if I’m not satisfied with the cleaning?

Your satisfaction is our top priority. If anything was missed, contact us within 24 hours of your appointment and we will send a team back to re-clean the area free of charge.
