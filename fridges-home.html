Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer emergency refrigeration breakdown services?

Yes. We provide emergency breakdown callouts for refrigeration equipment, including cold rooms, commercial fridges, freezers and other refrigeration systems. Contact us for assistance.

Why is my air conditioner leaking water?

Your aircon is leaking water most likely due to a clogged condensate drain line, dirty air filters causing frozen coils, a damaged drain pan, or improper installation/leveling, leading to water overflow or inability to drain properly. While you can often clean filters and check for major blockages, persistent issues like low refrigerant or component damage require a professional technician to prevent mold and water damage.

What are the signs that my cold room needs repair?

Common indicators include inconsistent internal temperatures, unusual noises (buzzing or grinding), frost or ice buildup on walls or coils, condensation on the inside of the room, and food spoiling before its expiration date.
