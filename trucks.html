Frequently Asked Questions

What brands of refrigerated units do you service?

We service and repair all major commercial transport refrigeration brands, including Thermo King, Carrier Transicold, Zanotti, and Daikin. Whether you operate a single refrigerated sprinter van or a full fleet of 53-foot reefer trailers, we have the diagnostic tools and parts to help.

How often should a refrigerated truck be serviced?

We recommend a comprehensive preventative maintenance inspection every 1,000 to 1,500 operating hours, or every 3 to 4 months depending on usage. Regular servicing prevents costly compressor failures and extends the lifespan of your unit.

Can you come to our yard to service multiple trucks at once?

Yes. We offer on-site fleet maintenance programs. Our mobile technicians can visit your depot or warehouse during off-peak hours or weekends to service multiple vehicles at once, eliminating driver downtime.

How quickly can a technician reach my broken-down truck?

For emergency calls within our primary service area, our average dispatch arrival time is between 60 and 90 minutes. When you call, we will give you a real-time ETA based on your exact location.
