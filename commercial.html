Frequently Asked Questions

What brands and types of equipment do you work with?

We install and repair all major commercial brands. Our expertise covers upright storage fridges, under-counter units, glass-door display fridges, deli counters, walk-in chillers, blast chillers, and ice machines.

How quickly can a technician get to my business?

For emergency breakdowns, we aim to have a certified technician on-site within [Insert Your Timeframe, e.g., 2 to 4 hours] depending on your location. For standard installations and routine servicing, we schedule appointments at a time that minimizes disruption to your business.

How often should commercial fridges be serviced?

We recommend professional preventative maintenance at least twice a year (every 6 months). High-heat environments like busy commercial kitchens may benefit from quarterly checks to prevent dust and grease buildup on condenser coils.
