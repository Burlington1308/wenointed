Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my air conditioner serviced?

We recommend servicing your air conditioning unit at least once a year, ideally right before summer starts. Regular maintenance keeps your warranty valid, improves air quality, and prevents costly breakdowns.

How do I know if I should repair or replace my old AC unit?

If your unit is over 10–12 years old, requires frequent repairs, or causes your electricity bills to spike, replacing it is usually more cost-effective. Our technicians will always give you an honest assessment of both options so you can decide what fits your budget.

What brands of air conditioners do you install and repair?

We service and repair all major residential and commercial brands. For new installations, we supply and install top-rated, energy-efficient units from trusted manufacturers.

How long does a new air conditioning installation take?

Most standard residential split-system installations take between 3 to 5 hours. Larger ducted systems or commercial projects may take 1 to 2 days, which we will outline clearly in your quote.
